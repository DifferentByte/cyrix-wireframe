End-to-end purchase flow

Requirement / PI→ AI spare match→ Technical verification→ New item (if needed)→ PR · history · sourcing→ Quotes & comparison→ New vendor (if needed)→ PO & approval→ Advance / UTR→ Follow-up & tracking→ GRN + invoice OCR→ Field acknowledgement

All screens

Create purchase request

Item or service PR with BEMP purpose, ticket/barcode, image and SO check.

Spare verification

Field engineer uploads a part photo; AI matches it to SAP items and runs checks.

Technical verification

Technical team compares images, history and equivalents, then approves or routes to ITR.

New item creation

Request, approve and generate SAP item codes for parts not in the master.

PR workbench & sourcing

Previous purchase history, rate contracts, and AI vendor discovery with RFQ send.

Quotations & comparison

Captured quotes, auto comparison sheet, vendor history, selection and approval.

New vendor request

Collect KYC documents and forward to Finance for a vendor code.

PO creation & approval

Generate the PO, route it through the value-based approval matrix, and push to SAP.

Advance & payments

AP down payment request or invoice, payment status and UTR sync.

Open POs & delivery

Open PO dashboard, automatic reminders, dispatch and tracking.

GRN & invoice OCR

Warehouse receipt with invoice scan, OCR extraction and PO matching.

Field acknowledgement

Mobile screen for engineers to confirm receipt with photo and location.

Warranty, CMC & PM

Warranty register and claims, CMC renewals, and PM report collection.

Vendor performance

Scorecards, delivery and quantity performance, and flags below 60%.

90-day stock forecast

AI forecast from 3-month consumption, shortfalls, and automatic stock PRs.

Ledger reconciliation

Quarterly vendor ledger vs SAP vs PO, invoice, payment and UTR.

Zones & categories

Zone-wise allocation to executives, plus Sales, Admin, IT, Quality and Calibration PRs.

Management dashboard

PR, PO, payment, delivery and vendor dashboards for project in-charges.
