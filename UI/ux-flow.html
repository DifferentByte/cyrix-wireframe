UX principles applied

  • 1Show images next to item codes wherever a part is picked, so the wrong part isn't ordered
  • 2Put history (previous POs, ITRs, vendor score) beside every decision
  • 3Present AI output as a suggestion for a person to confirm
  • 4Block invalid paths early: CAMC part indents, work orders without an SO
  • 5Keep one stage bar on every screen, so users always know what comes next
